## Scientific checks and exact results

**Decision contract and mechanism.** The paper distinguishes daily capacity K=2, mean planning target rho=0.5, target-hour review slots, and separate process/hour value. The basic additive-hour cost threshold is elementary and is not claimed to optimize early process coverage. Process reward requires an actual event hour within [onset,onset+6 hours), with each process rewarded once. Active-day clustering admits one inactive-day bridge, splits on missing days, and flags both missing-data and evaluation-edge uncertainty. It is an evaluation definition, not a measured meteorological episode label.

On the common 725-day/17,400-hour core sample there are 2,105 q90 event hours and 71 processes. Native Top2, ordinary gate and inherited priority N/H/C values are **1450/341/35**, **618/322/32**, and **572/259/34**. The ordinary gate is an exact subset of Top2 under identical forecasts, eligibility and ties. All 832 removed slots are accounted for: **19 event and 813 non-event**. The 94.4% figure is 322/341 retention of Top2 hits; total event-hour recall is 322/2105=15.3%. The three lost early processes and weather/severity details are retained, including the January French excess of 5,705 MW explicitly restricted to early joint-event hours.

**Fair policy contrasts.** The p/risk-rise × cooldown on/off comparison shares gate 0.25, capacity and full planning history. The previous gate 0.19 priority remains separately named. Top1, Top2, static, rolling and no-review policies share the appropriate forecast domain. The rolling gate uses the preceding 90 already-issued forecast-complete days, a 30-day warm-up and a 0.25 fallback; it cannot read future labels or future forecasts. Policy masks are generated before applying the truth-completeness scoring mask. Forecast and cooldown history through 2022–2025 precedes 2024–2025 scoring. Ordinary gates and controllers permit zero selections.

**Costs, workload and uncertainty.** Full cost curves include all competitive native policies and no review, with U_process/v=C−rN; hourly-hit utility is separate. The elementary fixed-copula coupling bound is not sold as a new theorem.
